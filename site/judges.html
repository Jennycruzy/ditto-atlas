For judges: 60 seconds, empty wallet

  1. In PayBox, ask:
    Ditto: find where real people put USDC on Base today.
  2. Then:
    Ditto: dry run plan 1 with 1 USDC.
    Simulated with balances overridden; nothing is signed.
  3. Then:
    Ditto: explain 0x48c2353ddc7b815454489bdac3ed343e53650f1aaa1854d1197d3cd2fafaabb2 on Base.
  4. Open Proofs and press "re-check now": four real runs, 1 USDC in and all of it back, read again from the chain in your browser.
